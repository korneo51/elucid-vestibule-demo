## Layout contract

- Hall master: 1672 × 941. CSS/JS portal points are measured in this coordinate system.
- Both door masters: 1024 × 1536, centered seam at x=512. CSS displays one master twice, clipping its left and right halves at 50%. The image is never permanently split into separate files.
- The room previews are independent images behind each door's modal. Text and calls to action remain HTML.
- Generated illustrations are fictional atmospheres, not exact depictions of the playable rooms.
